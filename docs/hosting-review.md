# Hosting review — preliminary notes

Prepared by: Taylor Chen (`ChunkLoader`)  
Status: in progress; comparison due October 27, 2026.

## Background
Performance complaints started after the September 18 community night. Several busy sessions with 25–30 players showed lower-than-usual ticks per second. The upcoming Great Overworld Build-Off may bring another burst of activity before Season 5.

## Options being compared
| Option | Potential upside | Concern |
|---|---|---|
| Keep current host, optimize settings | Least disruptive | May not solve peak-hour limits |
| Move to larger instance | More headroom | Higher recurring cost |
| Change hosting provider | Different hardware / network | Migration risk before Season 5 |

## Decision notes
Alex has not approved a migration. Taylor favors finishing the economy test first unless performance deteriorates. The technical review on October 18 ended without a production move date.
